Support

Stuck, found a bug, or a purchase didn't arrive? Email galaxygrove.support@gmail.com with your iPhone model and what happened. We reply within a few days.

Purchases

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. On a new device, open the Star Shop and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your save

Your farm is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes the save.
