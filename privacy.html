Privacy Policy

Galaxy Grove · Last updated 2 November 2026

Galaxy Grove is made by Devin Hutchison. There are no accounts, and your farm is saved on your own device.

Ads. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, it is provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (not the advertising ID), approximate location (from your IP address) and how you interact with the ad, to show it, measure it and prevent fraud. We ask Google for non-personalized ads only and the app never asks to track you. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change it from the Star Shop ("Ad privacy choices").

Game analytics. To learn which parts of the game players enjoy and where they get stuck, the game sends anonymous play events (for example "finished tutorial step 4", session length, purchases made, crashes) to GameAnalytics. They're tied to a random id made by the game, not to your name, Apple ID or advertising ID, and are never used for advertising. GameAnalytics' policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Purchases. Apple handles all payments. We never see your payment details.

Game Center. If you're signed in, your Tower of Power best floor is shared on Apple's Game Center leaderboard under your Game Center name.

Children. The game is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
